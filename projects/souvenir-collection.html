Brand Memorabilia

The Physical Form of Inspiration

Brand Memorabilia is a tangible extension of brand culture and a supplementary expression of brand narrative. It translates abstract concepts, visual identity, and emotional value into everyday objects that can be touched and used — serving as an emotional bridge between the brand and its audience beyond functional purposes.

It currently encompasses two directions:

Everyday Aesthetics — focusing on tactile quality and daily usability, with brand visual cues woven naturally into real-life settings.

Annual Gift Collection — focusing on scenario-based experiences and the ritual of gift-giving, conveying the brand's understanding of specific use cases through thoughtful curation and packaging design.

Brand memorabilia carries no narrative pressure from the main product line, nor does it pursue excessive expression. They exist quietly in the interstices of daily life — with measured design interventions, they complete a light, effortless dialogue between the brand and its people.

01
Everyday Aesthetics

Quiet Companionship

The memorabilia closest to us are often the least conspicuous. They don't mind the occasion, need no explanation, and ask for no attention. They simply settle into the corners of daily life — on collars, in backpack pockets, on fridge doors, within arm's reach on a desk.

Yet in this quiet companionship, the brand achieves its most profound connection: not to be seen, but to be owned; not to be remembered, but to be used.

This is the unique power of portable objects — turning the brand from something you see into something that stays with you.

02
Annual Gift Collection

Time Capsules — One Box a Year

One box a year. Different every year.

It took five years to realize that each box is a time capsule — not of products, but of where the company stood and what we wanted to say to our recipients that year.

Lined up together, the five boxes show little consistency. Paper, fabric, steel, tea leaves, circuit boards — materials shift, directions turn, every year is different. And that very difference is the true hallmark of this series. We never followed a fixed template; instead, we asked ourselves anew each year: What did we do this year? What do we want to say? What best represents us right now?

The answers were never the same.

2022

Riding the Waves

New Year Box — objects to post, flip, and tear off. Each flip, a small farewell and a new beginning.

2023

On-the-Go Nap Care

From paper to fabric — a complete napping set. A moment to close your eyes in peace.

2024

Field Mini Toolkit

Our gaze turned outdoors. Each item hints at "heading out." Let's go outside.

2025

Xinuo · IoTea

Come back for a cup of tea. IoT meets the tea garden — a box that releases the scent of the hills.

2026

Mega Evolution

Three layers, opened one by one. Ritual, practicality, and tech, all inside.

Five years on, the contents have changed endlessly, but the reason never has:

At the end of every year, we offer a tangible token of gratitude, on behalf of the brand, to everyone who has walked alongside us —
Thank you. See you next year.

03
Deconstruction Special

Mega Evolution — Crossing Boundaries

Mega Evolution is a gift about crossing boundaries — between the physical and the digital.

The shell is transparent, revealing its structure at a glance: tea on the top layer, a link card in the middle, and a cup at the bottom. Like an exploded view of a product, opening is deconstruction, and deconstruction is reading.

The lines on the packaging draw from two sources: the human symbols on the 1977 Voyager Golden Record, and the circuit traces of a PCB. One points to the cosmos, the other to the digital world.

The green patterns transform circuit traces into the shapes of mountains and water — where cold industrial structure meets Eastern natural imagery, and PCB coexists with tea in a single box.

Each of the three layers has its role: tea offers taste, the link card enables connection, and the cup provides a vessel.

Setting out from the physical world, journeying through the digital, and returning to the physical — a complete crossing, all contained in one box.

These are our attempts over the years to turn inspiration into reality.
