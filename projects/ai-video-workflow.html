Overview

AI-Powered Video Content Production Pipeline

Video content has become the core vehicle for brand communication and product marketing, yet traditional video production involves planning, filming, editing, and post-production — high cost, long cycles. Jimeng Seedance 2.0, as a next-generation AI video generation tool, dramatically lowers the barrier to video creation through text/image-driven video generation capabilities.

This workflow solution is tailored for Seeed Studio's brand video needs, organizing the complete process from creative ideation, prompt design, and iterative generation to post-production integration — helping teams rapidly produce brand promos, product showcases, event recaps, and more.
